INTRO

There are two King Balls. They can convert other balls to their own color when they collide. The two King Balls battle against the rest. When the King Balls collide with each other, they shrink, while the other balls grow larger and turn black. If either King Ball converts more than 80% of the balls to its color, you lose. If you make both King Balls disappear and turn all other balls black, you win.
